# ⚙️ Workflow / Pipeline

1. **Frame Extraction**
   - Extracts frames from the gameplay video at 24 FPS.
2. **Object Detection (YOLOv8)**
   - Detects agents, weapons, and environment.
3. **Narrative Creation (LLM)**
   - Summarizes game events using LLaVA.
4. **Commentary Generation**
   - Produces concise tactical commentary.
5. **(Optional) TTS & Video Stitching**
   - Adds voice commentary and merges frames into a final video.
